# Research log — KMT–CCP ties, media ownership, PRC education (2026-10-01)

**Asked for by Jing:** enrich the data with ties between the KMT and the CCP.
Specific leads:
- trips and meetings
- the media ties of 盧秀燕's sister
- 旺旺 / 蔡衍明: their news channels and PRC links, and whether 旺旺 made
  political donations
- a KMT figure who studied in the PRC

**Method:** web searches in Chinese and English, plus direct page reads where
a detail mattered. Every edge added from this log cites the URLs below in its
`evidence`, with `retrieved_date: 2026-10-01`. Edges stay
`review_status: 'draft'` until someone reads each source and adds a verbatim
quote.

**Caution on excerpts:** most come from search-engine summaries of the linked
pages, not from reading the pages in full. Excerpts marked **(read)** come from
fetching the page itself. Re-read the source before citing anything publicly.

**Coverage bias, stated plainly:** this round researched only KMT–CCP ties, at
Jing's request. The rules for recording a tie are identical for every party.
What isn't identical is how much has been looked for. Cross-strait contacts by
DPP, TPP or independent figures (for example 柯文哲 at the Shanghai–Taipei
城市論壇) have **not** been researched yet. Until they are, the map will show
more KMT–PRC lines partly because more were searched for.

## 4. 旺旺 / 蔡衍明 / 中天 / 中時 / 中視

**Ownership:**
- 蔡衍明 bought the China Times group (中國時報, 中天, 中視) in November 2008
  and renamed it 旺旺中時媒體集團.
  - Sources: [天下 5001838](https://www.cw.com.tw/article/5001838),
    [Lingua Sinica dispatch](https://lingua-sinica.org/dispatch/want-want-china-times-media-group/)
- **中視 (TWSE 9928):** listed group is 旺旺, with directors' and
  supervisors' holdings at **51.20%**.
  - Source: [Money-Link 9928 profile](https://ww2.money-link.com.tw/TWStock/StockBasic.aspx?SymId=9928).
    The primary record would be TWSE's 公開資訊觀測站, which wasn't checked.

**PRC government subsidies:**
- 中國旺旺控股's annual reports record subsidies "從各(中國)政府機構收到的補貼收入，作為給予本集團在中國若干附屬公司的獎勵".
  - 2017–2018 financial year: **RMB 477 million**.
  - 2017 plus Q1 2018: RMB 624 million.
  - 2016: RMB 368 million.
- 旺旺's own explanation: these are local-government investment incentives
  (招商引資).
- Sources: [PTS 429749](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/429749),
  [PTS 234785](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/234785),
  [Taiwan News 3686080](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/zh/news/3686080)

**Regulator and courts:**
- **NCC, 2020-11-18:** the commission voted 7–0 to refuse renewal of 中天新聞台's
  licence. It cited 25 penalised violations and major shareholders intervening
  in news production. 中天 left cable on 2020-12-12 and moved to YouTube.
  - Sources: [PTS 636160](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/636160),
    [PTS 636194](https://news.pts.org.tw/article/636194),
    [TechNews 2020-11-18](https://technews.tw/2020/11/18/ctitv-change-license-fail/)
- **臺北高等行政法院, 2023-05-10:** the court overturned the NCC refusal because
  the NCC had used an improperly adopted scoring table, and sent the case back
  for re-review. The NCC said it would appeal; the appeal's outcome was **not
  researched**.
  - Sources: [TVBS 2120066](https://news.tvbs.com.tw/politics/2120066),
    [NOWnews 6135882](https://www.nownews.com/news/6135882)

**Reported editorial direction from the TAO (allegation):**
- **Financial Times, July 2019:** the FT reported that 中時 and 中天 editors
  received calls from the TAO on coverage angle and front-page placement.
- 旺旺 sued the FT, then **dropped the suit on 2021-03-11**.
- Sources: [Wikipedia: China Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Times),
  [Taiwan News 3746341](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/en/news/3746341),
  [Taipei Times 2021-03-12](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2021/03/12/2003753690)
- ~~Not added to the data.~~ **Added 2026-10-01** after Jing approved a new
  relation type, `reported_editorial_direction` (spec decision 19). There are
  two edges, `china-times-reported-tao-direction` and
  `ctitv-reported-tao-direction`. Both are **T4** (a reported allegation,
  denied by the subject), with status `disputed`, and hidden on first load.
  The FT article's own URL wasn't captured, so its evidence entry has
  `source_url: null`.

**Paid placements (Reuters, August 2019):**
- Reuters found the TAO paid at least five Taiwan media groups for coverage,
  with contracts it reviewed.
- Sources: [VOA mirror](https://www.voanews.com/a/press-freedom_paid-news-china-using-taiwan-media-win-hearts-and-minds-island-sources/6173678.html),
  [New Bloom](https://newbloommag.net/2019/08/17/reuters-report-media-outlets/)
- **Not added:** Reuters withheld the outlets' names, so the finding can't be
  attached to any node.

**Unification statements:** per Wikipedia **(read)**, 蔡衍明's 2018
「無色覺醒」 proposals include 「台灣人就是中國人」. Not encoded: a statement
isn't a tie in our vocabulary.

## 7. 旺旺 political donations: not found

- Searched in Chinese, including an extended search. **No report** of 旺旺,
  its subsidiaries or 蔡衍明 donating to the KMT or its candidates turned up.
- **This is absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.** The authoritative
  place to check is the Control Yuan's 政治獻金公開查閱平臺
  ([ardata.cy.gov.tw](https://ardata.cy.gov.tw/)). It is search-driven and
  wasn't queryable from here. A manual search by donor name (旺旺, 中國時報,
  中天, 中視, 蔡衍明) is the next step.
- **Relevant law (read):** 政治獻金法 §7, last amended 2018-06-20. It bars
  donations from 「大陸地區人民、法人、團體或其他機構，或主要成員為大陸地區人民、法人、團體或其他機構之法人、團體或其他機構」,
  and the equivalent for Hong Kong / Macau and foreign entities. (A search
  summary also claimed a 20% PRC-capital threshold. That wasn't in the verbatim
  text read, so it isn't relied on.)
  - Source: [全國法規資料庫 政治獻金法](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=D0020049)
